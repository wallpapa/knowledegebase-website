# Selection

The owner selected the mouth-corner and DAO question from first-party consultation experience. This run did not claim it was the highest-traffic service.

Three bounded Thailand mobile SERPs were collected: two Thai and one English. The recurring decision needs were:

1. identify whether the visible concern is muscle pull, missing support or lower-face laxity;
2. compare DAO Botox Program with Filler, Thread Lift and Energy-Based Tightening Programs;
3. ask for product brand, units and the itemised total;
4. understand assessment timing, expected duration and follow-up.

These needs were added as concise patient questions above the comparison list.
