# Bright Data disposition

- Accepted as search-intent evidence: cause, suitability, alternatives, filler versus thread, units, total price, duration and review timing.
- Rejected as medical authority: result snippets, clinic claims, rankings, promotional language and treatment-duration numbers.
- Public copy uses the existing reviewed medical sources. Search evidence only changed the questions answered and the words patients use.
- Program naming is enforced as `DAO Botox Program` in English and `โปรแกรมโบท็อกซ์ DAO` in Thai.
