## How Many Sessions?

Most patients need 6–8 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart. Hair grows in cycles, and laser only targets hairs in the active growth (anagen) phase. This is why a single session cannot treat all hairs simultaneously.

## What Does It Feel Like?

Most patients describe the sensation as a rubber band snapping against the skin. The Gentle Max Pro's built-in DCD cooling system sprays cryogen immediately before each laser pulse, significantly reducing discomfort. For sensitive areas, topical numbing cream is available on request.

## Pre & Post Treatment Care

**Before**: Shave the area 24 hours prior. Avoid waxing, plucking, or epilating for 2–4 weeks. Avoid sun exposure and self-tanner for 2 weeks.

**After**: Apply moisturizer and SPF 30+. Avoid hot showers, saunas, and intense exercise for 48 hours. Do not exfoliate for 1 week. Shaving between sessions is fine; do not pluck or wax.

## Who Should Avoid?

- Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals
- Active skin infection or open wounds in the treatment area
- Recent tanning (within 2 weeks)
- Photosensitizing medications

## FAQ

### Is laser hair removal truly permanent?
Laser hair removal provides significant and long-lasting hair reduction. However, hormonal changes may stimulate new follicle growth over time. Annual maintenance sessions help sustain results.

### Can I do just one session?
One session treats only the hairs currently in the active growth phase (approximately 20–30% of total). Multiple sessions are needed to target all hairs through their growth cycles.

### How do I verify the machine is genuine?
Ask the clinic to show the manufacturer's label and FDA registration. A legitimate clinic will not hesitate to confirm this.
