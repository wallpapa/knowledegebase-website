## Why devices cost less here
Procurement, staffing and rent structures differ. The same 5th-generation Thermage or Ultraformer treatment — official treatment tip, standard shot count — costs noticeably less at a licensed Bangkok clinic. The condition: verify authenticity (treatment-tip serials, clinic authorization).

## Where "cheap" is a trap
- Filler priced below brand guidance: may be split-dosed, grey-market or expired product
- Suspiciously low surgical packages: anaesthesia and aftercare billed separately
- No named doctor — just "Korean team" or "expert team"
