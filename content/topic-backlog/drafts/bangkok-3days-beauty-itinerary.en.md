**Direct answer:** A 3-day Bangkok treatment trip realistically fits: Day 1 consultation + one non-invasive treatment (Thermage/Ultraformer/skin booster), Day 2 rest or light review, Day 3 check-up and fly home. After any injectable filler, wait 48 hours before flying.

## Why only 1–2 big treatments?
Tissues need recovery time. Cramming multiple treatments means swelling overlaps and you can't tell which one worked.

- VERZO CLINIC: ~350 m walk from BTS Phra Khanong

## Budget frame (excluding flights/hotel)
One energy-device treatment + one injectable = a sensible ceiling for most first trips. More requires a doctor's recovery plan.
